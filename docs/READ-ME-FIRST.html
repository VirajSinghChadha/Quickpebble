READ ME FIRST

Opening Quick Pebble for the first time

It takes about a minute, and you only do it once.

Why will my Mac show a warning? Quick Pebble is free and open source, but it isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate yet. macOS therefore asks you to confirm that you trust it. This is normal for apps from independent developers, and you stay in control: nothing opens until you say so.

1 · Install

1

Drag Quick Pebble to Applications

Drag the Quick Pebble icon onto the Applications folder in this window. Then eject this disk image.

2 · Allow it in Privacy & Security

For macOS 15 Sequoia and newer. On older versions, see the section below.

1

Try to open it

In Applications, double-click Quick Pebble.

What you'll see

“Quick Pebble” Not Opened — Apple could not verify it is free of malware.

Done  ← click this (not “Move to Bin”)

2

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security

Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner, choose System Settings, then Privacy & Security in the sidebar.

3

Scroll down to “Security” and click Open Anyway

Security
“Quick Pebble” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

Then enter your Mac password or use Touch ID.

4

Confirm once more

Click Open Anyway in the final window. Quick Pebble opens, and from now on it opens like any other app.

The Open Anyway button appears for about an hour after your first attempt. If you don't see it, double-click Quick Pebble again, then go back to Privacy & Security.
macOS 14 Sonoma or older

In Applications, hold Control and click Quick Pebble (or right-click). Choose Open, then click Open in the warning window.

It says the app is “damaged and can't be opened”

That's the same safety check shown differently. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Quick Pebble.app"

Then open Quick Pebble again.

3 · Permissions it may ask for later

Only the Screen assistant mode needs these, and only if you use it. Both are in System Settings → Privacy & Security:

  • Screen & System Audio Recording — lets the assistant see your screen.
  • Accessibility — lets the assistant move the mouse and type for you.

Switch Quick Pebble on in each list. The browser works fully without them.

Is it safe? Quick Pebble is open source (MIT); every line is public at github.com/VirajSinghChadha/Quickpebble. Only download it from there. Your saved passwords are encrypted with a master password that only you know.
Prefer Homebrew? brew tap VirajSinghChadha/quickpebble https://github.com/VirajSinghChadha/Quickpebble && brew install --cask quick-pebble clears this warning for you.
